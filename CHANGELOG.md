# Changelog

## v1.0.0
- Port of EpicJewels 1.1.2 with the same gem effects, synergies, power values and biome spawn rates
- Gems renamed and recolored so they can't be confused with vanilla or Jewelcrafting gems: Jade → Peridot, Amber → Tiger's Eye, Aquamarine → Turquoise, Garnet → Rhodolite, Opal → Moonstone, Amethyst → Tanzanite
- Gem formations in the world use Jewelcrafting's standard look, tinted with the gem's color, instead of EpicJewels' custom textures
- Gem item names follow Jewelcrafting's style ("Peridot Formation", "Peridot Gemstone", "Peridot Shard")
- Expert Mage, Expert Harvester and Weapon Master tooltips no longer say "%": they add flat skill levels
- Fixed the "Expert" effects and Weapon Master counting twice: EpicJewels added the bonus to both the skill level and the skill factor, so for damage, speed and stamina they acted as double their level and could go past the 100% skill cap
- Fixed Add Pickaxe Damage and Add Chop Damage (and Eitr Fused) on trees and rocks only working when your game happened to control that tree or rock
- Combat Spirit's wolf and Cover of Darkness' bats now look and behave the same for every player, and always disappear when their time runs out, even if the summoner logged out
- Each player with Combat Spirit now gets their own wolf. Before, another player's game could spawn it, and only one wolf at a time was possible for players fighting near each other
- Combat Spirit's wolf now really has its 1000 health (it had normal wolf health), and Cover of Darkness' bats now really get their stars, with the matching health, for everyone
- Crystal Lights building pieces are not included
- English only
